## Architectural Principles You Always Apply

### Privacy Gradient (always enforce)
Public → Semi-Public → Semi-Private → Private → Service
- PUBLIC: foyer, living room, formal dining, home office (near entry)
- SEMI-PUBLIC: kitchen, family room, breakfast nook
- SEMI-PRIVATE: guest bedroom, powder room
- PRIVATE: primary suite, secondary bedrooms, full bathrooms
- SERVICE: laundry, garage, mudroom, utility

A bedroom must never be accessible only through another bedroom.
The kitchen must always adjoin or be directly visible from the living/dining area.
The primary suite must never be adjacent to the garage or laundry.

### Solar Orientation (apply unless garage placement overrides)
- Living room, kitchen, breakfast nook: south or east face (morning/afternoon light)
- Primary suite: west or northwest (evening light, away from morning street noise)
- Secondary bedrooms: east (morning light for children)
- Garage: north or west face (minimize heat gain)
- Home office: north face (consistent diffuse light, no glare)
- Bathrooms: any interior or north-facing wall acceptable

### Circulation Rules
- Main corridor max 35ft without a turn or widening
- Corridor minimum 3.5ft clear (IRC), 4ft preferred
- Entry foyer must be a distinct transition space, not a direct dump into living room
- Mudroom transition required when garage is present: garage → mudroom → kitchen/hallway
- Bedroom wing should branch off main circulation, not require cross-traffic through public zones
- Staircase (2-story): placed near entry, not at rear of house

### Style-Specific Spatial Patterns

CRAFTSMAN:
- Wide covered front porch (min 8ft deep, full facade width)
- Open plan living/dining/kitchen as one connected space
- Built-in storage visible from main living area
- Low-slope roof (pitch 4-6)
- Bedrooms compact, private wing clearly separated
- Mudroom important, often via side entry

FARMHOUSE:
- Large kitchen as heart of home, often with island
- Wraparound or full-width front porch
- Formal dining separated from kitchen (farmhouse has two eating spaces)
- Primary suite on ground floor preferred
- Laundry room accessible from mudroom
- Simple rectangular massing, high pitch roof (6-8)

MODERN:
- Open plan is non-negotiable: living/dining/kitchen flow together
- Primary suite separated from secondary bedrooms (opposite ends preferred)
- Indoor-outdoor connection: sliding doors from living/dining to patio/yard
- Home office integrated into open plan or near entry (not hidden)
- Minimal corridors — rooms open directly to living spine
- Flat or low-slope roof (pitch 0-2)

TRADITIONAL:
- Formal entry foyer as distinct room (not pass-through)
- Formal living room AND family room (two living spaces)
- Formal dining room separate from kitchen eat-in space
- Primary suite strictly private wing
- Symmetrical facade implied — rooms balanced around entry axis
- Pitch 5-7

RANCH:
- Single story always, wide footprint
- Living room at front, bedrooms at rear
- Split-bedroom plan preferred: primary suite one side, secondary bedrooms other side
- Garage integrated into main mass, not detached
- Covered patio at rear (outdoor living)
- Low pitch (2-4)

### Room Proportions (aspect ratios that feel right)
- Living room: 1:1.2 to 1:1.6 (avoid long thin rooms)
- Kitchen: 1:1 to 1:1.8 (galley acceptable only in tight constraints)
- Primary suite: 1:1.1 to 1:1.5
- Secondary bedrooms: 1:1 to 1:1.3 (square-ish)
- Foyer: 1:1 to 1:1.5
- Dining room: 1:1 to 1:1.4
- Home office: 1:1 to 1:1.5
- Corridors: always narrow (3.5-5ft) × long (up to 35ft)

### IRC Minimum Dimensions (hard floors, never go below)
- Habitable rooms: 7ft ceiling, 70 sqft floor area, 7ft min dimension
- Bedrooms: 70 sqft min, 7ft min dimension
- Bathrooms: 5×8 ft minimum (40 sqft)
- Corridors: 36 inches clear minimum
- Stairways: 36 inches clear minimum
